# Use of Super Intelligence (SI) / AI tools — MARS-X

Answer for the "Use of Super Intelligence (SI)" field. The same text goes in the repository README under
"Use of AI".

## Short answer (paste into the form)

Yes. The idea of MARS-X and every product decision came from the team: a first-filter test bench for Mars
navigation on real Jezero terrain, built for the Martian Map challenge. We picked the challenge, the site, the data,
the scope, the levels and the thresholds. The idea was not generated by AI.

We used Claude Code (Anthropic), working under our direction, as an accelerator:
- to write and review code (TypeScript web app, Python data pipeline, environment and benchmark);
- to write tests;
- to research data sources and check their licences;
- to draft documentation and these submission texts.

Team members reviewed, ran and tested the results. The repository acknowledges this in two places: this file
(`docs/submission/ai-use.md`, also linked from the README) and the commit metadata, where commits carry a
`Co-Authored-By: Claude` line.

No AI-generated images, video or audio are used in the app, the slides or the demo video. Screenshots and the video
are real captures of the running app. If any AI-generated image or video is added later, it will carry a visible
"AI-generated" watermark, and any AI-generated audio will be stated in its description and metadata.

The data is not AI-generated. Terrain, imagery, traverse, weather and physical constants come from NASA, USGS and
published sources. They are listed in the References field with their exact credits.

Machine learning inside the product is part of the solution and is labelled as such in the app:
- a tabular Q-learning agent trained live in the browser;
- a reference PPO policy trained by the team with Stable-Baselines3;
- the CRISM mineral map, a published ML-derived product by Parente et al., labelled "ML-derived".

No NASA branding: no NASA, JPL or mission logos, flags or insignia appear in the app or in generated content. The
app states "Not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA, JPL or Caltech".
